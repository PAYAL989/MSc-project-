Canavalia gladiata Project — Work Done From Start to End

1. **Plant material collection** – Collected *Canavalia gladiata* leaves and cleaned them properly.

2. **Leaf preparation** – Washed, dried, and powdered the leaves.

3. **Leaf extract preparation** – Prepared the aqueous leaf extract using distilled water under controlled temperature and time.

4. **Copper solution preparation** – Prepared the required concentration of copper sulfate (CuSO₄) solution.

5. **Nanoparticle synthesis** – Added the plant extract to the copper sulfate solution to initiate green synthesis of copper nanoparticles.

6. **pH adjustment** – Used NaOH solution to adjust the reaction conditions and support nanoparticle formation.

7. **Centrifugation** – Centrifuged the synthesized mixture to separate the nanoparticle particles.

8. **Washing and purification** – Collected and washed the nanoparticle pellet to remove unwanted substances.

9. **Drying and calcination** – Dried the nanoparticles and subjected them to calcination at controlled temperature.

10. **UV-Visible characterization** – Characterized the synthesized nanoparticles using **UV-Visible spectroscopy**.

11. **Antibacterial activity** – Evaluated antibacterial activity against selected bacterial strains such as *E. coli*, *S. aureus*, and *S. typhi*.

12. **Result analysis** – Recorded, analyzed, and interpreted the experimental results to confirm nanoparticle synthesis and antibacterial potential.
